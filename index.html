What I learned building this

What I understand better

I understand how one attendee list can act as the source of truth for the whole page. When someone checks in, I update that list and recalculate the total, each team’s turnout, and the progress toward the goal. This keeps the numbers in sync instead of maintaining separate counters that could drift apart.

What I could explain in an interview

I could explain how the form submit event prevents a page reload, validates and normalizes a name, and adds an attendee object with a team and check-in time. I would also describe how the interface is re-rendered from that data and how localStorage restores it after a refresh. I’d note that localStorage is suitable for this demo, but not shared across multiple check-in devices.

Real-world demo scenario

At the summit entrance, a volunteer enters an employee’s name, selects Water Wise, Net Zero, or Renewables, and checks them in. The attendee list, team leaderboard, and progress toward 50 update immediately. In a real multi-kiosk event, I would replace device-only storage with a shared, access-controlled service so every station sees the same attendance.
